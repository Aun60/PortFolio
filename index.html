// about

From spreadsheets to
autonomous systems.

I graduated with a BS in Artificial Intelligence from FAST NUCES Karachi, and my work sits across three connected roles: Data Analyst, AI/ML Engineer, and Full Stack Developer. My path started in raw data, cleaning it, modeling it, and reporting it, and has grown into building the systems that reason over it: retrieval pipelines, multi agent workflows, and the full stack products that put them in front of real users.

As a Data Analyst I turn messy spreadsheets into dashboards stakeholders actually trust. As an AI/ML Engineer I design RAG pipelines and agentic systems that stay fast and reliable under real constraints, not just in a notebook. As a Full Stack Developer I take those systems from a first prototype to a product people can actually open and use. I like problems that sit at the seam between these disciplines, and I'm comfortable owning a project from a raw CSV all the way to a production deploy.
